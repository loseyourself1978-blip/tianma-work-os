# TWOS 1.0.5 · Tian action voice

Nyra plays short, bundled Chinese recordings by Tian at action transitions: a rest reminder on reclining and a cheer on verified success. Sound starts muted. Unmute and enable Action voice in Sound; close the panel with Close, Escape or Sound again.

## Enable, preview and close

Nyra expanded menu or stage toolbar → Sound → Chinese → “Tian · bundled cues”. Unmute, enable Action voice and adjust Volume. Choose an Action phrase and press Preview.

Close via the visible Close button, Escape or the Sound button again. Closing keeps action voice enabled. Mute stops all sound; disabling Action voice stops automatic phrases while manual previews remain available.

Tian supplies the fixed Chinese phrases. English continues to use an installed macOS female voice, not an English Tian recording. Installed voice choices remain available. Existing custom cue imports take priority; mute, volume and language preferences are preserved.

## Contextual cues

Greeting; recline/rest; start working; await confirmation; verified success; encouragement after failure; outfit change; wave/hover. Each cue has its own fixed recording and phrase. Chinese examples: “累了吗？喝口水，休息一下吧。” and “欧耶！你好棒！”.

One phrase per eligible transition, no continuous loop. Global cooldown: 10 seconds. Same cue: 45 seconds; rest/wave: 120 seconds. No overlapping speech or deferred playback of events observed while hidden/muted. Startup and reconnection do not replay old work/success. Partial, cancelled, unverified or disconnected states never celebrate. Manual Celebrate is a preview, not business acceptance.

## Source, licensing and privacy

The selected source is [TTSMaker](https://ttsmaker.com/zh-cn), voice 1413, free default style. No Pro emotion setting, subscription or purchase is used. Only fixed public phrases are submitted during production; never task/chat/private content. The generated recordings are bundled in both installers. Runtime playback is offline and makes no synthesis requests.

The [commercial audio license](https://ttsmaker.com/copyright_and_commercial_license_terms/zh-cn) permits use, modification, copying and distribution of generated audio, including commercial use. Provenance includes the voice ID, phrases and each file's SHA-256. This is not an exclusive voice or model ownership claim. The vendor's sample recording is not used as an action cue.

## Import and reset

Choose a language and action → Import → select a decodable recording under 25 MiB and at most 120 seconds. Import remains local; errors preserve the original clip. Reset removes only this language/action's custom recording. With Chinese Tian selected, it returns to the matching bundled cue. Legacy generic imports remain Greeting-only. Missing, corrupted or mismatched bundled audio reports an error instead of silently substituting another voice.
