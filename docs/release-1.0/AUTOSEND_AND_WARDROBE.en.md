## DeepSeek automatic relay (TWOS)

After ordinary chat, enable “Automatically send replies to…” once beside the current complete reply. Waiting for the other pane or reconciling a receipt does not erase the user's enabled intent. A real pause keeps its reason and requires explicit Resume after the condition is resolved; paused is not sending.

Unchecking either pane, host Stop automatic send, or either website's native Stop revokes future dispatch on both sides first. Already-sent content is not claimed to be withdrawn. An interrupted partial reply has no Send control; Continue generating remains the website's own action. Refresh/restart retains receipts without automatically re-enabling a stopped exchange.

When a new conversation replaces its composer, the checkbox is mounted by the current visible editor, not a hidden old one. Unknown delivery is reconciled before any retry; do not work around it by manually sending a duplicate.
## New outfits and sound

The Outfit selector adds White blouse and Ruby gown after the preserved Black & purple, Office and Gala entries. All five have standing and reclining runtime art. The new pair uses transparent frames for idle/blink, working, reading, waiting, looking up/around, celebration, and matching-outfit rise → directional run/jump → settle. Disabled optional actions have no art and never substitute another outfit. The new directional runs are two-frame loops, not eight-frame animations.

Sound → Chinese → Tian bundled cues, or English → local female voice. Unmute and enable Action voice. Chinese has 13 fixed offline Tian recordings; English has 13 corresponding phrases using an installed local female voice (Samantha en-US verified on this Mac), not English Tian recordings. If no English voice is available, the panel reports it instead of playing Chinese as English.

Idle, reading, moving, jumping and settling join the preserved eight cues. One phrase starts on an eligible transition, with cooldown and no overlap; it does not repeat on every frame or status poll. First connection, reconnection and old snapshots do not replay audio. Partial, cancelled and unverified work never celebrates. Manual previews remain labelled previews and do not change task truth.

Close, Escape or the Sound button closes the panel without disabling action voice. Mute or hiding stops playback. Existing per-cue imports, volume, language and outfit preferences are preserved.
