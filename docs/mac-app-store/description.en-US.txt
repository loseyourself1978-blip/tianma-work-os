Lead your AI team. Keep the work moving.

TWOS brings supported AI conversations into one Mac workspace, so you can explore ideas side by side and move from discussion to useful results with less copying and switching.

COMPARE IDEAS, KEEP THE CONTEXT
Work in two connected conversation panes. Compare approaches, develop a draft, or ask another assistant to challenge an idea.

HAND OFF WITHOUT THE COPY-PASTE LOOP
Send a complete reply to the selected conversation. Enable automatic reply sharing for a supported pair, and stop future sends from either side. Waiting, pauses, and delivery problems are shown clearly.

KEEP YOUR FILES IN SCOPE
Choose the text and supported attachments you intend to share. Inspect available results and use the file actions supported by the submitted version. Selecting a folder is not permission to read everything in it.

MEET NYRA
Nyra is your bundled animated desktop companion, with five outfits, a transparent character stage, and read-only awareness of supported task activity. She cannot send messages, run commands, or approve work.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Use your own accounts for compatible AI services. Provider availability, model access, usage limits, subscriptions, and data practices remain subject to each provider's terms. TWOS does not include unlimited third-party AI access.

Built for Apple Silicon Macs. Internet access and compatible provider accounts are required for online AI features.
